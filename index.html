How it's scored

Every answer is a stage from 1 to 5

Each question has five answers, written as a ladder. Stage 1 represents mostly manual and disconnected work. Stage 5 represents connected, continuously improved practices with advanced automation or AI where appropriate. Pick the one that matches most of your flights today.

Each area gets an average

The four answers in a section are averaged, giving a score between 1.00 and 5.00. Your overall score is the average of the five areas.

The two lowest are always shown

These are where the next gains are. If two areas tie, the one that affects safe, on-time flying more comes first: operations, then data, change, AI, and passenger channels.

The shape picks your profile

The rules below are checked in order, and the first one that fits is your profile. Nothing is weighted or hidden.
